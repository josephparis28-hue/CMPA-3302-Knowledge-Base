## **Definitions**

Clear, practical definitions for commonly used terms in hands‑on technical work:

### Tool

A physical device used to perform a specific task, applying force, precision or control.

### Method

A repeatable procedure or sequence of actions used to complete a task.

### Technique

A specific way of performing a method to improve safety, efficiency or accuracy.

### Maintenance

Actions taken to preserve the condition, performance and lifespan of a system or tool.

### Preventative Maintenance

Tasks performed to reduce the likelihood of future failures or damage.

### Corrective Maintenance

Repairs or adjustments made after a problem has been identified.

### Workflow

The ordered sequence of steps required to complete a task from start to finish.

### Inspection

A visual or physical evaluation used to identify wear, damage or abnormalities.

### Diagnostic

A process used to identify the cause of a problem through testing, observation or measurement.

### Hazard

Any condition, object or situation that poses a risk of injury or damage.

### PPE (Personal Protective Equipment)

Gear worn to reduce exposure to hazards (gloves, goggles, respirators and etc.).

### Tolerance

The acceptable range of variation in a measurement or fit.

### Load

The amount of force, weight or electrical demand applied to a system or component.

### Material Behavior

How a material responds to force, heat, moisture or environmental conditions.
