This template provides a structured workflow for diagnosing electrical issues safely and effectively.

## 1. Observe

- Burn marks or discoloration

- Melted insulation

- Buzzing or crackling sounds

- Burning odors

- Flickering lights or intermittent power  

## 2. Test

- Measure voltage with a multimeter

- Check continuity on wires and components

- Inspect breaker status

- Test outlets and switches  

## 3. Isolate

- Turn off circuits individually

- Disconnect suspect components

- Separate loads from the circuit  

## 4. Diagnose

- Identify failed components

- Determine overload vs. short circuit

- Check for loose or corroded connections  

## 5. Correct

- Replace damaged components

- Tighten connections

- Reset or replace breakers  

## 6. Verify

- Restore power

- Retest voltage and continuity

- Confirm stable operation
