This template provides a structured workflow for diagnosing mechanical issues.

## 1. Observe

- Wear, cracks, or misalignment

- Grinding, squealing, or rattling

- Vibration or heat buildup  

## 2. Test

- Move components manually

- Check torque and tension

- Test bearings, belts, and gears

- Measure temperature or vibration  

## 3. Isolate

- Remove belts or accessories

- Test components individually

- Disconnect power or load  

## 4. Diagnose

- Worn or failed parts

- Lubrication issues

- Alignment or tension problems  

## 5. Correct

- Replace worn components

- Lubricate moving parts
  
- Realign or retension assemblies  

## 6. Verify

- Reassemble

- Test under load

- Confirm smooth operation
