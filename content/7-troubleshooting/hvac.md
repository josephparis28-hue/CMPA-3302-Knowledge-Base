This template provides a structured workflow for diagnosing HVAC performance issues.

## 1. Observe

- Weak airflow

- Unusual noises

- Ice buildup or condensation

- Temperature inconsistencies  

## 2. Test

- Measure vent temperature

- Check thermostat operation

- Test capacitor and motor function

- Inspect filters and coils  

## 3. Isolate

- Turn off HVAC zones

- Disconnect blower or compressor

- Test components individually  

## 4. Diagnose

- Airflow restrictions

- Electrical vs. mechanical failure

- Refrigerant‑related symptoms  

## 5. Correct

- Replace filters

- Clean coils

- Repair electrical components

- Adjust or replace motors  

## 6. Verify

- Restore system

- Measure temperature again

- Confirm stable airflow
