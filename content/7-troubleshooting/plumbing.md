This template provides a structured workflow for diagnosing plumbing issues.

## 1. Observe

- Leaks or moisture

- Hissing or gurgling

- Low water pressure

- Slow drains or backups  

## 2. Test

- Run water through fixtures

- Check shutoff valves

- Test pressure at multiple points

- Use a drain snake or inspection camera  

## 3. Isolate

- Shut off water to specific fixtures

- Block or isolate drain sections

- Remove suspect components  

## 4. Diagnose

- Clogs, leaks, or pressure issues

- Pipe damage vs. fixture failure

- Venting problems  

## 5. Correct

- Clear clogs

- Replace seals or washers

- Repair or replace damaged pipes

- Adjust or replace fixtures  

## 6. Verify

- Restore water

- Check for leaks

- Confirm proper flow and pressure
